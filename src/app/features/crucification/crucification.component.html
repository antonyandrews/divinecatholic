The Passion Of Christ

Crucifixion Of Jesus Christ

A sacred and heartbreaking journey through the suffering, sacrifice, betrayal, agony and divine love of Jesus Christ from Gethsemane until the crucifixion at Golgotha.

Gethsemane — Late Night

The Prayer Of Blood And Agony

After the Last Supper, Jesus entered the Garden of Gethsemane with His disciples.

The night was silent, yet the weight of humanity’s sins pressed heavily upon Him.

Knowing the suffering awaiting Him, Jesus fell to the ground and prayed:

“Father, if it be possible, let this cup pass from me. Nevertheless, not my will, but Yours be done.”

His sorrow became so intense that His sweat turned like drops of blood.

Christian tradition sees this moment as the beginning of Christ’s suffering — where divine love accepted the pain of redemption.

Gethsemane

The Sleeping Apostles

Jesus asked Peter, James and John to remain awake and pray with Him.

Yet when He returned, He found them sleeping repeatedly.

The loneliness of Christ deepened. Even His closest companions could not fully understand the magnitude of His suffering.

“Could you not watch with Me one hour?”
Betrayal

Judas Betrays Jesus

Judas Iscariot, one of the twelve apostles, arrived with Roman soldiers and temple guards.

For thirty pieces of silver, Judas agreed to identify Jesus.

In the darkness, Judas approached Christ and kissed Him.

“Greetings, Rabbi.”

The kiss became one of history’s most painful symbols of betrayal.

Jesus responded not with hatred, but with sorrow and compassion.

“Friend, do what you came to do.”
Arrest Of Jesus

The Healing Of The Soldier’s Ear

During the arrest, Peter drew a sword and struck the servant of the high priest, cutting off his ear.

Chaos erupted among the crowd.

Yet Jesus immediately intervened.

“Put your sword back in its place.”

Jesus touched the servant’s ear and healed him instantly.

Even while being arrested, Christ demonstrated mercy, peace and divine compassion.

Roman Trial

Jesus Before Pontius Pilate

Jesus was brought before Pontius Pilate, the Roman governor.

Religious leaders accused Him of claiming to be king.

Pilate questioned Jesus directly:

“Are You the King of the Jews?”

Jesus answered calmly:

“My kingdom is not of this world.”

Pilate found no crime deserving death. Yet political pressure and the raging crowd pushed him toward condemnation.

Public Judgment

The Crowd Chooses Barabbas

Pilate offered the people a choice: release Jesus or release Barabbas, a known criminal.

The crowd shouted violently:

“Crucify Him!”

Pilate symbolically washed his hands, declaring himself innocent of Christ’s blood.

Yet the sentence was approved.

Roman Punishment

The Scourging Of Jesus

Roman soldiers brutally whipped Jesus.

The scourge contained sharp fragments designed to tear flesh.

Blood covered His body as soldiers mocked Him mercilessly.

They placed a crown of thorns upon His head, forcing the sharp thorns deep into His skin.

“Hail, King of the Jews!”

They spat upon Him, struck Him repeatedly and clothed Him in a purple robe to mock His kingship.

The Fourteen Stations Of The Cross

Station I — Jesus Is Condemned To Death

Pilate officially sentenced Jesus to crucifixion. Though innocent, Christ silently accepted the judgment.

Station II — Jesus Takes Up The Cross

Roman soldiers forced Jesus to carry the heavy wooden cross toward Golgotha.

Station III — Jesus Falls The First Time

Weak from blood loss and torture, Jesus collapsed beneath the cross.

Station IV — Jesus Meets His Mother

Mary saw her Son suffering. Their silent exchange carried unimaginable pain.

Station V — Simon Helps Carry The Cross

Simon of Cyrene was forced to help Jesus carry the cross.

Station VI — Veronica Wipes The Face Of Jesus

Veronica compassionately wiped Christ’s wounded face.

Station VII — Jesus Falls The Second Time

Exhaustion overwhelmed Him again, yet He continued onward.

Station VIII — Jesus Speaks To The Women

Jesus comforted grieving women despite His own agony.

Station IX — Jesus Falls The Third Time

Near Golgotha, Christ collapsed once more beneath unbearable suffering.

Station X — Jesus Is Stripped Of His Garments

Soldiers removed His clothing publicly, deepening His humiliation.

Station XI — Jesus Is Nailed To The Cross

Massive iron nails pierced His hands and feet while crowds watched in horror.

Station XII — Jesus Dies On The Cross

Darkness covered the land as Jesus surrendered His spirit.

“Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do.”

Station XIII — Jesus Is Taken Down From The Cross

Mary received the broken body of her Son with sorrow beyond words.

Station XIV — Jesus Is Laid In The Tomb

Jesus was placed inside the tomb, yet hope remained hidden within the silence of death.

Golgotha

The Crucifixion Of Christ

Jesus was nailed upon the cross between two criminals.

Roman soldiers mocked Him, gambled for His garments and challenged Him to save Himself.

One thief insulted Him, while the other defended Him and sought mercy.

“Jesus, remember me when You come into Your kingdom.”

Jesus answered:

“Today you will be with Me in paradise.”

Despite unbearable suffering, Christ prayed for forgiveness for those crucifying Him.

“Father, forgive them.”

Finally Jesus cried:

“It is finished.”

He surrendered His spirit, and the earth trembled.
