Divine Timeline

Miracles Of Jesus Christ

A sacred chronological journey through the divine miracles performed by Jesus Christ from His birth until His crucifixion and resurrection.

Approx. 4–6 BC Age: Birth Bethlehem

The Star Of Bethlehem

At the moment of Jesus Christ’s birth, a mysterious and radiant star appeared across the night sky. Wise men from the East followed the heavenly light believing it marked the arrival of the Savior.

The divine star guided them toward Bethlehem, where Infant Jesus rested with Mary and Joseph. The miracle revealed heaven itself announcing the birth of Christ to humanity.

Matthew 2:1–12
Approx. 4–6 BC Age: Newborn Near Bethlehem

Angels Appear To Shepherds

Shepherds watching over their sheep witnessed the night sky suddenly filled with divine light and angels.

Heaven proclaimed peace and salvation through the birth of Jesus Christ. Fear turned into worship as ordinary people became the first witnesses of the Savior’s arrival.

Luke 2:8–20
Approx. 8 AD Age: 12 Jerusalem Temple

Jesus Amazes Temple Scholars

At only twelve years old, Jesus astonished religious teachers with wisdom far beyond ordinary human understanding.

Scholars listened in amazement as the young Jesus interpreted sacred scriptures with divine clarity.

Luke 2:41–52
Approx. 27 AD Age: 30 Cana, Galilee

Water Into Wine

During a wedding celebration, the hosts ran out of wine.

Jesus instructed servants to fill jars with water. When poured out, the water had miraculously transformed into fine wine.

This became the first public miracle of Jesus Christ.

John 2:1–11
Approx. 27 AD Age: 30 Sea Of Galilee

The Great Catch Of Fish

Fishermen struggled the entire night without catching anything.

Jesus instructed Peter to cast the nets once more.

Instantly the nets overflowed with fish, revealing divine abundance and the power of obedience.

Luke 5:1–11
Approx. 28 AD Age: 31 Sea Of Galilee

Calming The Storm

A violent storm threatened to destroy the disciples’ boat.

Jesus stood and commanded the sea:

“Peace, be still.”

Immediately the winds ceased and the waters became calm.

Mark 4:35–41
Approx. 28 AD Age: 31 Sea Of Galilee

Walking On Water

During the night, Jesus walked across the sea toward His frightened disciples.

Nature itself submitted to His authority, revealing divine power over creation.

Matthew 14:22–33
Approx. 29 AD Age: 32 Near Bethsaida

Feeding The 5,000

Thousands gathered to hear Jesus speak, yet only five loaves and two fish were available.

Jesus blessed the food and it multiplied miraculously until everyone was fed.

Matthew 14:13–21
Approx. 29 AD Age: 32 Jerusalem

Healing The Blind Man

Jesus restored sight to a man blind from birth.

This miracle became a symbol of spiritual enlightenment and divine mercy.

John 9:1–12
Approx. 30 AD Age: 33 Bethany

Raising Lazarus

Lazarus had been dead for four days.

Jesus approached the tomb and commanded:

“Lazarus, come forth.”

The dead man emerged alive, demonstrating Christ’s authority over death.

John 11:1–44
Approx. 30 AD Age: 33 Golgotha

Darkness During Crucifixion

As Jesus hung upon the cross, supernatural darkness covered the land.

The earth trembled and the temple veil tore apart from top to bottom.

Creation itself mourned the suffering of the Son of God.

Matthew 27:45–54
Approx. 30 AD Age: 33 Jerusalem

The Resurrection

Three days after crucifixion, the tomb was found empty.

Jesus appeared alive to His followers, bringing hope, eternal life and victory over death.

Matthew 28:1–10
